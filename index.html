About

My research interests lie in genetics and computational biology. I use single-cell and spatial genomics, generative representation learning, and interpretable computational models to study cellular development and disease.

Education and academic training

I received my Bachelor of Medical Sciences from Peking University in July 2026, completing a five-year program.

Overall GPA: 3.71/4.0 · Academic rank: 9th of 98 · Final defense: 3rd of 98

From April to August 2025, I was a Visiting Student at Yale University in the Departments of Genetics and Urology, advised by Dr. Siyuan (Steven) Wang and Dr. Ping Mu. From July to October 2026, I worked as a Remote Research Intern in Yale’s Department of Genetics with Dr. Monkol Lek.

Previously, I trained as an Undergraduate Research Intern in the Department of Immunology at Peking University with Dr. Qing Ge from June 2022 to July 2026.
