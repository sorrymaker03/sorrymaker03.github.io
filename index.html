I’m Mingke Wu, a medical sciences graduate of Peking University. My research brings together single-cell and spatial omics, generative representation learning, and interpretable modeling to understand cellular development and disease.

Through research at Yale University and Peking University, I have worked on pediatric skeletal muscle development, spatial transcriptomics, cancer lineage plasticity, and drug-response programs in multiple myeloma.

01 / Development & representation learning

Mapping pediatric skeletal muscle development.

Building a developmental muscle atlas from 60 pediatric skeletal muscle single-cell multiome datasets spanning infancy to adolescence, using deep generative latent representations with MultiVI and MrVI.

Multiome integrationMultiVI · MrVICross-species transfer
Biological focus
Fibro-adipogenic progenitors (FAPs)
Question
How do cell composition and function change during pediatric development?
Approach
Latent representation learning, pathway analysis, and cross-species alignment
Research details
  • Identified age-associated compositional remodeling, including increased CD55+ and decreased GPC3+ FAPs.
  • Investigated functional remodeling and FAP–MuSC signaling programs during maturation.
  • Developed a representation-alignment and transfer-learning pipeline to validate age-associated shifts across external human and mouse datasets.

PRISM-MM

Interpretable modeling

A sparse integrative dictionary learning framework that separates conserved drug-response programs from study-, drug-, and cell source-specific variation in multiple myeloma.

SCAPE

AI-assisted analysis

An AI-driven platform for comprehensive single-cell data analysis, bringing R and Python workflows together to study cross-species cancer metastasis and microenvironmental adaptation.

04

SPADE

Spatial transcriptomics

An R package for spatial proximity analysis of differential expression, connecting gene-expression changes to the spatial organization of cells.

Additional research
  • AI agent-based analysis of RAEFISH data from mouse liver, lymph node, and placenta, including spatial zonation and cell interactions.
  • Genetic perturbation prediction in spatial transcriptomics using State, CPA, and scLAMBDA.
  • LLM-guided cross-model analysis of prostate cancer lineage plasticity, investigating E2F1 and HMGB2.
  • Clonal profiling of MGUS-to-MM progression, tricuspid valve disease, and 3D models of extramedullary multiple myeloma.
